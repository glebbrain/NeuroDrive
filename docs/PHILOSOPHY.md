# NeuroDrive Philosophy

**You are the character. Real life is the game.**

NeuroDrive rewards real progress instead of fictional character strength. The aim is to help people develop useful abilities, complete real goals, and enjoy the process.

## No pay-to-win
No purchasing XP, achievements, skill ratings, boosts, or competitive advantages. Donations, if enabled, provide no gameplay benefit.

## Three distinct progressions
- **GLOBAL:** engagement and contributions to the NeuroDrive project; gamified but resistant to repetitive farming.
- **CURRENT:** progress toward a specific real-world goal, with user-selectable or custom professional pathways.
- **PERSONAL:** evidence-based development of capabilities, including attention, learning, planning, and recovery. A game score is not automatically proof of real-world cognitive transfer.

XP indicates reward history, not measured competence. Personal skill ratings must be based on appropriate tests and interpreted cautiously.

## Sustainable progress
Music can help users begin work, but intense focus should not require skipping food, sleep, movement, or rest. Recovery prompts are configurable and respectful; breaks and absence are not punished.

## Respect for people
All data is local by default. Sharing is opt-in. Avoid manipulative retention tactics, forced streaks, exploitative monetization, and arbitrary social comparison.

## Open collaboration
The source code is licensed under GNU GPLv3. Community members can submit issues and pull requests. User-provided music is never distributed with the application.
